# Acme Robotics Annual Report 2024

## 1. Introduction
Acme Robotics was founded in 2009 in Pune, India. The company designs warehouse automation systems. Priya Sharma has served as Chief Executive Officer since 2018. The headquarters moved to Bengaluru in 2015.

Acme operates three business divisions: Logistics, Healthcare, and Agriculture. The Logistics division is led by Rahul Verma, and employs 420 people.

## 2. Financial Results
Revenue grew steadily across all regions during the fiscal year. Table 1 summarizes revenue by region, and Figure 1 shows the growth trend over three years.

Table 1: Revenue by region (USD millions).
| Region | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 |
|---|---|---|---|
| APAC | 120.5 | 148.2 | 187.9 |
| EMEA | 98.3 | 110.7 | 125.4 |
| Americas | 140.0 | 152.6 | 161.3 |

The strongest growth came from APAC, driven by demand for warehouse robots from e-commerce customers.

## 3. Operations
The Logistics division opened a new fulfilment robotics plant in Chennai in 2023. The plant produces 1,800 units per year.

The Healthcare division is led by Meera Iyer and works with 37 hospital partners. Healthcare robots assist with medication delivery and sample transport.

Agriculture is the smallest division with 85 employees. It focuses on autonomous crop monitoring.

## 4. Risks
Supply chain disruption is the most significant operational risk, because key actuators are sourced from a small number of suppliers. Currency fluctuations affect reported revenue in the EMEA and APAC regions.

Regulatory changes in healthcare robotics could delay product approvals. The company manages these risks through supplier diversification, currency hedging, and early engagement with regulators.
